# Francesco Politano

I am a solutions architect. I take a business requirement, in the words the customer used, and turn it into something that can be built, run and paid for. Lately that mostly means simplifying a process with AI.

## The job

I work in presales at Corley Cloud, in Turin. A company describes a problem, usually a process that costs more than it should or a system that will not scale where the business is going. My part is to understand it well enough to say whether AI or cloud is the right answer at all, design the architecture, size the effort and the running cost, and stay with the project once it is under way. In practice: AI assessments, agentic architectures on Amazon Bedrock, retrieval over document sets nobody has time to read, cloud migrations. Before this I spent five years on the engineering side of the same company, first as team leader and then as Head of Cloud.

## The part I do for myself

Agentic systems are where my own curiosity goes. What I have built so far runs my working day rather than anyone's product: a dashboard fed by the CRM, an event log, persona panels that argue with a document before it reaches a customer, a handful of MCP servers and skills. It is private code written for one user, and I keep it honest by using it every day. As pieces of it become useful to someone other than me, I will publish them here.
